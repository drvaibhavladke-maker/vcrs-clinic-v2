The Pathway

Every case follows the same complete process — no shortcuts.

A lesion that looks similar to another can have a completely different diagnosis under the microscope. This is why each step is followed in full before a treatment approach is recommended.

STAGE 1

Examination

A thorough clinical and radiological examination of the oral cavity — noting the site, size, and character of any lesion, swelling, ulcer, or growth.

STAGE 2

Diagnosis

Findings are correlated with clinical presentation to reach a provisional diagnosis, using standardised classification and grading where relevant.

STAGE 3

Investigations

Where needed — biopsy and histopathological analysis, hematological workup, or imaging — to confirm the diagnosis with certainty rather than assumption.

STAGE 4

Treatment

A treatment approach suited to the confirmed diagnosis, explained clearly, with counselling and follow-up built into the plan.
